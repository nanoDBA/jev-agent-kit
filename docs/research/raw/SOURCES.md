# Source snapshots

`docs/research/` are our own summaries; where they mention a page, read it at the source:

- Index of all pages: https://docs.typesafe.ai/llms.txt
- API reference: https://docs.typesafe.ai/api.md
- Models: https://docs.typesafe.ai/models.md

The notes were written against the pages as published on 2026-09-25 and may have drifted since.
